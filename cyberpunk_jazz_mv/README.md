## Animation use

Import PNGs with straight/unmatted alpha. Preserve the generated transparency; the dark background shown in CONTACT_SHEET.jpg is preview only.

Assets are flattened individual cutouts, ready for position, scale, rotation, opacity, glow and parallax animation. They are not rigged models or separated mechanical parts. Butterfly wing flapping requires a mesh deformation or additional wing separation in the animation editor.

Suggested layering: skyline in the far background, individual buildings in the midground, signage and lighting closer, instruments and decorative motifs in the foreground, FX above those layers. Animate spotlight and beam independently. Use butterfly and star on their matching chorus lines. The waveform ribbon is decorative, not a measured representation of the track.

CONTACT_SHEET.jpg is an overview. The numbered category folders contain the final individual PNGs. Original reference files remain untouched and are not bundled.
